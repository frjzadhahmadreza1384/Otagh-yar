# Otagh-yar
This is a private project for the Urmia University of Medical Sciences and Health Services of West Azerbaijan Province, Iran.
OtaghYar (ir.umsu.otaghyar) is the official student dormitory booking and roommate coordination Android application for Urmia University of Medical Sciences (umsu.ac.ir). It enables verified students to select eligible dormitories and rooms, coordinate same-gender roommates via invitation codes, and track their housing requests in real time under the supervision of the university's dormitory administration.
